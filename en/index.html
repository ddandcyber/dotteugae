FAQ

Is it really free? Do I need an account?

Yes. Every feature is free and there's no sign-up. Your work is saved only in the browser on your own device.

Can I sell patterns I make?

Your patterns are yours. All the fonts in the tool are free fonts that allow commercial use, so you can sell lettering patterns made with them. Just don't sell patterns that use someone else's work, such as characters or brand logos.

Can I keep working on another device?

In the pattern maker, tap Save to download a pattern file (.json). Open that file with Open on another device and pick up right where you left off.

How many stitches is one cell?

One cell is one single crochet stitch, and each horizontal line of cells is one row. The finished size depends on your yarn and hook, so check your gauge first (see the gauge guide, in Korean).
