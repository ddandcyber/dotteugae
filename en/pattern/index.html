      Free alpha pattern maker for crochet

      Crochet Alpha turns a grid into a crochet chart. Paint cells by hand, or type a word, pick one of 18 pixel fonts and get an alpha pattern in seconds. Each cell is one stitch and each line of cells is one row, so it works for tapestry crochet, single crochet colorwork and other pixel crochet projects. Save your chart as an image or a pattern file — free, no sign-up.

      • Paint: tap or drag over cells. Fill: colors a whole connected area of the same color at once.
      • Tap the selected paint color again (or right-click a color) to change or remove that palette slot. Cells you've already painted keep their color.
      • Current row counts from the bottom as row 1 and is outlined on the grid.
      • The background is the color of unpainted cells, so changing it later keeps your pattern intact.
      • In Text to pattern, choose a Shadow direction to lay a shadow color behind the letters. Set the style to Glitch (two colors) to shift two colors in opposite directions. Outline wraps the letters by one or two cells, corners included.
      • Drag the text and let go to lock it in place. Placed it wrong? Tap Undo.
      • Rotate turns the whole pattern 90° or 270° clockwise — handy when working rows vertically.
      • Add words one at a time and move each to stagger lines.
      • Text flow makes letters wave, bounce, or climb up and down diagonally.
      • Make your own decorations with Draw new decoration in the Decor tab, or select something on the canvas and tap Add to my decorations.
      • Use the Select tool to drag a box around placed text or decorations to move them again — each connected blob can be moved on its own.
      • Copy and Paste floating text, decorations or selections as many times as you like. Keys: Ctrl+C · Ctrl+X · Ctrl+V · Delete · Esc · Enter · arrows
      • After adding text, tap Move letters to pick and move each letter freely — great for fine-tuning a wave layout.
      • While moving text, tap ↔ Center · ↕ Center to center it on the grid.
      • Save image downloads a PNG file right away. On a phone it may go to your Downloads folder instead of your photo gallery. To add it to your gallery, tap Share next to it and save from there.
      • Use the arrow button at the right end of the toolbar (below it on phones) to collapse it so the pattern fills the screen. Tap the small arrow handle at the top right to bring it back. Keyboard: Ctrl+\ (Cmd+\ on Mac)
